# What Is Agile (Without the Buzzwords)?

Picture two ways to plan a surprise dinner for friends.

**Way one:** You plan every dish, every guest, every song, and every napkin for a month. On the night, you reveal everything at once. If someone is vegetarian, you find out when it's too late.

**Way two:** You cook one dish and ask a friend, "Does this look good?" Then you adjust and cook the next one.

Way two is **agile.**

## The Big Idea

Agile means building something **in small pieces**, showing it to real people **early and often**, and **changing the plan** based on what you learn.

It started in software, where requirements change faster than anyone can predict. Instead of guessing everything up front (the older "waterfall" way), teams work in short cycles and adapt.

## Waterfall vs Agile

- **Waterfall:** Plan everything, build everything, and show it at the end. Great when nothing changes. Risky when things do.
- **Agile:** Plan a little, build a little, learn a lot, and repeat.

Neither is evil. If you're building a bridge, you want a plan. If you're building an app people haven't tried yet, you want agile.

## The Values (In Plain English)

The original Agile Manifesto values:

- **People** and conversations over rigid processes
- **Working things** over mountains of documents
- **Working with customers** over arguing about contracts
- **Responding to change** over following a plan blindly

It doesn't say "no plans." It says the stuff on the left matters more when you have to choose.

## What It Looks Like Day to Day

- Work is split into small **stories** (things a user wants).
- The team works in short cycles called **sprints** (often 1 to 4 weeks).
- Every sprint ends with something **working** that people can try.
- The team regularly asks, "What should we do differently?"

## Agile Is Not...

- **Not "no planning."** It's lots of small plans.
- **Not "go fast and break things."** It's about learning fast, not being sloppy.
- **Not only for software.** Marketing teams, nonprofits, and even families use these ideas.
